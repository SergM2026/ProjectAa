## The Opportunity

Hawksoft agencies hire talented professionals — many without an insurance background — and face extended ramp-up periods, knowledge inconsistencies, and reduced confidence in client interactions. There is no standardized, Hawksoft-specific training solution in the market today.

**AgencyVA has already built one.** Our Agency V University LMS delivers structured, role-ready training across all major lines of insurance — built entirely around the Hawksoft platform. Together, we can put this solution in the hands of every Hawksoft agency.

## Two Partnership Paths

### Option A: Subscription-Based LMS Access (Recommended)

AgencyVA provides Hawksoft and its agency partners with turnkey access to Agency V University.

**One-Time Onboarding**
- Enterprise setup and administrative configuration
- User provisioning and leadership orientation
- Success planning and rollout support

**Monthly Subscription (Per User)**
- Full LMS access with all Hawksoft-specific training modules
- Comprehensive insurance curriculum (Personal Lines, Commercial Lines, coverage principles, terminology, agency workflows)
- Guided learning paths with testing at every progression step
- Live instructor-led workshop sessions
- Ongoing platform updates aligned with Hawksoft releases
- Administrative visibility into employee progress and engagement

**Why this works for Hawksoft:** Zero development burden. Hawksoft offers a proven training solution to agency partners, strengthening retention and platform stickiness — without building or maintaining anything.

### Option B: Collaborative Curriculum Build

AgencyVA provides expertise and guidance to help Hawksoft develop internal training capabilities.

- Shared curriculum frameworks and content strategy
- AgencyVA consulting on module design and best practices
- Hawksoft owns and maintains the resulting materials

**Why this works for Hawksoft:** Full ownership and control of training content, with AgencyVA's expertise accelerating development.
